# v1.0.7 Hotfix 7.10.1 — Lineup Pool Disclaimer

Small player-facing clarity update layered on top of Hotfix 7.10.

## What changed
- Adds a small informational note directly below saved picks on the normal lineup home screen and Review My Five.
- Explains that a player ruled OUT before Saturday at 11:59 PM ET may be replaced in the available pool.
- Explicitly reassures players that **saved picks will never change automatically**.
- Reminds players to check back before the Sunday 1:00 PM ET lock if a starter is affected.

## What did not change
- No lineup mutation logic changed.
- No pool replacement logic changed.
- No scoring, lock, backup, ticker, NFL refresh, or Commissioner behavior changed.
- No database migration or AWTRIX update is required.

## Included cumulative work
This package supersedes the previously prepared Hotfix 7.10 package and includes the Two-Speed Live Sunday Engine as well as this disclaimer update.
