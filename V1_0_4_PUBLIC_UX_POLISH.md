# Teal's Sunday Pick'em — v1.0.4 Public UX Polish

**Date:** September 10, 2026

## What changed

### First-time How to Play
New players now see a clearer first-time explainer before entering the app. It covers:
- the five-position lineup (QB/RB/WR/TE/K);
- the core half-PPR scoring rules, including passing, rushing/receiving, interceptions, and kicker scoring;
- Questionable-player emergency backups;
- the Sunday 1:00 PM ET lock and live/season standings;
- a reminder that the full rules remain available under **Profile → How to Play & Scoring**.

### Review My Five hierarchy
On a valid five-player review, **SAVE MY LINEUP** now appears immediately below the lineup summary. The five Change-position controls move below a divider under **Need to make a change?**, so phone users do not have to scroll past edit controls just to confirm their lineup.

## Explicitly unchanged
- live NFL parsing and scoring proven in the Wednesday full-game rehearsal;
- player matching and pool ranking;
- all scoring formulas, including every made FG = 3 points;
- lineup persistence / Select → highlight → Next behavior;
- Questionable/OUT emergency-backup activation rules;
- Sunday 1:00 PM ET lock;
- Supabase schema, auth, secrets, and GitHub Actions schedules;
- Commissioner live-test tooling.
