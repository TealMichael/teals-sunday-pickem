v1.0.5 — Tap-to-Edit Lineup Review

Review My Five is now much cleaner on mobile: each of the five player cards is itself tappable and opens that position for editing. The current choice remains highlighted in the picker and Return to Review comes back to the lineup review. The old bank of Change QB/RB/WR/TE/K buttons is gone, while SAVE MY LINEUP stays immediately below the five cards. Questionable emergency-backup context remains visible, and OUT starters can be tapped directly for replacement.

No scoring, NFL data, Supabase persistence, auth, lock, Commissioner, SQL, or workflow behavior changed.
